# Pursuit Desk pricing basis — internal draft

The public preview shows **proposed Farcelis prices**, not observed market rates or a final quote. Confirm delivery hours and scope before accepting a customer.

## Public offer on the draft page

| Offer | Proposed starting price | Included capacity |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fixed-scope pilot | $5,000 | One active pursuit, four weeks, kickoff, six working templates, one weekly review and brief |
| Ongoing desk | $7,500 per month | Up to two active pursuits, weekly cadence, current leadership view |

The client owns bid decisions, representations, pricing, final content, signatures, and submission. Proposal writing, specialist pricing, legal interpretation, and final compliance review require separate scope. A solicitation and workload review determines the final fixed fee. Rush deadlines, complex multi-volume responses, high amendment counts, and additional active pursuits change the quote.

## External price checks, October 2026

- [Contracting Intelligence Group](https://www.contractingintelligencegroup.com/federal-proposal-writing-services) publishes a review starting at $750 and compliance/enhancement sprints at $3,500 and $5,000. These are different services, but show a five-thousand-dollar fixed-scope purchase is within a publicly listed proposal-support band.
- [The Bid Lab](https://thebidlab.com/learning-center/how-to-answer-a-last-minute-rfp/) publishes $245 per hour with a 10-hour minimum, $300 per hour for smaller engagements, and fixed quotes after reviewing the RFP. A $5,000 pilot is roughly 20 hours at $245; a $7,500 desk is roughly 30 hours. These are benchmarks, not Farcelis cost estimates.
- [The Bid Lab's engagement guide](https://thebidlab.com/learning-center/outsource-proposal-writing-what-small-businesses-should-know/) describes per-bid fixed fees and monthly retainers as common models, with scope and deadline pressure driving price.

## Quote gate

Before issuing a statement of work, record the solicitation, submission date, number of volumes and requirements, active amendment count, client review capacity, expected meeting cadence, and specialist needs. Estimate Katalin and Nathan's hours separately. If the workload exceeds the included capacity, raise the fixed fee or narrow the scope. Track actual hours on the pilot, then revise the public starting prices if the economics require it.
